## Using AI to write and worried you sound very similar to everyone else?
--- 
<!--more-->

AI assistants are very useful tools. Particularly for helpingg with tasks where we're maybe lacking a bit in confidence or skillset. I don't have a great brain for numbers, so I like to use them to double check anything analytical or mathematical I work on.

Writing can be a challenging, time-consuming or potentially expensive task, so it makes sense that a lot of solopreneurs and small business owners use an AI assistant to help with drafting social posts and emails.  

### The problem: AI is trained on how 'people' write, not how 'you' write

The trade off with AI assistants is that they've been trained on very large models, which are very good at replicating the sum total of everything they've processed. 

This means they're well equipped to create something that 'ticks all of the boxes' in a very short space of time by using patterns.

Don't get me wrong, patterns are good, patterns are useful. But to cut through the noise, *your writing should be breaking patterns some of the time.*

### The solution: A writing style guide template to add to your prompts to get your assistant of choice to sound a bit more like you

One way I've looked at making AI assistants to sound a little less like each other is by treating them the same way an organisation or agency treats a copywriter. You don't just let them come in and write however they want, you give them a writing guide on how to sound like the company or brand.

So, I created a very boiled down version of one of these guides to include in a prompt any time you use AI to help write for your business.

### How it works

- Fill in each part of the document that has [square brackets]
- When you ask an AI assistant to write for you, attach the document to the prompt and include the phrase 'Ensure you follow the attached writing style guide.

The top half, which you'll fill in yourself, will give it enough information about how *you* want to sound. The bottom half is pre-filled (but can be added to if you'd like) and will steer it away from some of the usual AI-written tells.
